TechPulse Electronics Site Plan

Student Name: Agoro, Kelechi David | Business/Dev Brand: Mekaycee-Tech

Site Name

Site Name: TechPulse Electronics

Reason Selected: The name "TechPulse" combines technology and energy/momentum ("pulse")[cite: 1]. It signals a modern, dynamic web store dedicated to fast-moving electronics, genuine gadgets, and quick updates on products and tech specs for customers[cite: 1].

Optional Domain: techpulseelectronics.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of TechPulse Electronics is to provide a modern, easy-to-use e-commerce store where tech enthusiasts, students, and professionals can browse, search, and compare genuine laptops, smartphones, and accessories[cite: 1]. The site provides quick filtering, instant live search, clear specification details, dynamic local storage cart management, and dynamic tech news updates through external API integration[cite: 1].

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "I am a computer science student looking for a high-performance laptop within my budget. How can I filter products by budget and specs to find the best match quickly?"
  • Scenario 2: "I want to purchase a smartphone and an accessory. How do I add them to my cart, check total pricing, and ensure my selections stay saved even if I reload the browser page?"

Color Scheme

This site plan document uses the chosen project colors exclusively:

  • Deep Navy (#0d1b2a): Primary site color used for main page headers, site title background, footer, and primary call-to-action buttons.
  • Electric Blue (#0077b6): Accent color used for section headings, border accents, highlights, hover states, and dynamic links.
  • Off-White (#f8f9fa): Main page body background color to ensure strong visual contrast and soft viewing comfort.

Typography

The site uses two Google Fonts to keep the design clean and modern:

  • Heading Font: Montserrat (Bold, sans-serif) - Used for major section headers (H1, H2, H3) and product titles to give a clear, tech-focused look.
  • Body Font: Inter (Regular, sans-serif) - Used for paragraph descriptions, specs, lists, navigation elements, and form input controls for readability.
