NP Membership Benefits

Cost: Free

  • Basic listing in the online business directory.
  • Access to monthly networking breakfasts.
  • Community bulletin board posting privileges.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: $150 / Year

  • Full business directory listing with logo and web link.
  • Discounted tickets to annual business summits.
  • Quarterly newsletter business recognition.

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: $300 / Year

  • All Bronze level benefits included.
  • Eligible for Homepage Random Spotlight advertisements.
  • Free booth placement at annual chamber expos.

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: $500 / Year

  • All Silver and Bronze level benefits included.
  • Priority placement on Homepage Member Spotlights.
  • VIP sponsorship status for all community events.
  • Free quarterly digital marketing consultations.
